Data Safety Draft for Google Play Internal Testing

Data users may provide:
- Name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Case details
- Uploaded documents
- Payment references
- Support requests
- Legal SOS requests
- LawBot questions

Purpose of data use:
- Account access
- Legal-support coordination
- Advocate/client workflow
- Notifications
- Payment verification
- Audit logs
- Service delivery
- Case tracking and reminder support

Document processing:
Users may upload documents for legal-support coordination. Documents should be handled through the live Legal Connect platform and stored according to the backend configuration and privacy policy.

Payments:
Payment processing is handled through Razorpay. Legal Connect stores payment references and verification status for service coordination and Work Completion Hold.

Notifications:
Email notifications may be handled through Resend.

Legal disclaimer:
Legal Connect does not guarantee bail, case success, legal outcome, emergency rescue, police immunity, or any specific result. LawBot and platform information are source-based and informational. Final legal advice is subject to advocate consultation.
