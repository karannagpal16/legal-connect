This is an internal testing build of Legal Connect.

The Android app is a native WebView shell that loads the live Legal Connect web platform:
https://www.legal-connect.in

The backend health endpoint is:
https://www.legal-connect.in/api/health

Test credentials can be provided separately. Role-based login supports Client, Advocate, RNA/Admin, and Intern demo flows depending on backend configuration.

Payment is in Razorpay test mode only if the server is configured with Razorpay test keys. The app does not mark a booking as paid until backend signature verification succeeds. Work Completion Hold activates only after verified payment.

Legal Connect is a legal-tech and legal-support coordination platform. Information in the app is informational and not a substitute for legal advice from an advocate.
